Why post collateral

Your shares already do one job.

A tokenized share of NVIDIA gives you the price. It does nothing else. It sits there, and if you want cash you sell it and give up the position you wanted in the first place.

Keep the exposure

Borrow against the position instead of closing it. If the stock runs while you hold the loan, the gain is still yours. Selling to raise cash is a decision about the asset; borrowing is not.

No counterparty picking your price

Terms come from an oracle and a published calendar, not from a desk quoting you. The same rate applies to everyone in the pool at the same moment.

Capacity that admits what it is

Most lending assumes a live market to liquidate into. For a tokenized share that assumption is false for sixty hours every week. Pond prices those hours instead of pretending they are the same as Tuesday.

Check what an address unlocks

No wallet connection required. Borrowing against a volatile asset risks liquidation and loss of the collateral posted. The conservative closed-market terms reduce that exposure and do not remove it.

Instrument. Tokenized stock on Robinhood Chain is a tokenized debt security. It provides economic exposure to the price of an underlying security and confers no ownership, no voting rights and no legal claim against the issuer of that security. Holders carry the credit risk of the token issuer in addition to the price risk of the underlying asset.

Risk. Borrowing against volatile collateral carries the risk of liquidation and loss of the collateral posted. Overnight and weekend price gaps are a material hazard, and the protocol's conservative closed-window terms reduce that exposure without eliminating it. Smart contract risk and oracle risk apply. Pond has not been independently audited, and protocol-level supply and borrow caps are in place accordingly.

Availability. Tokenized equities are not available to users in the United States, and Pond restricts access accordingly. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, or an offer of any product in any jurisdiction.
