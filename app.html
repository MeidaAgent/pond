Instrument. Tokenized stock on Robinhood Chain is a tokenized debt security. It provides economic exposure to the price of an underlying security and confers no ownership, no voting rights and no legal claim against the issuer of that security.

Risk. Borrowing against volatile collateral carries the risk of liquidation and loss of the collateral posted. Overnight and weekend price gaps are a material hazard. Smart contract risk and oracle risk apply. Pond has not been independently audited, and protocol-level supply and borrow caps are in place accordingly.

Availability. Tokenized equities are not available to users in the United States. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
